Tempest Tumble physics (world pixels, 16 px tiles)

  kViewW/H     640 x 360  (integer scale into the window, letterboxed)
  kMaxSpeed    95 px/s
  kJumpVel    -210 px/s
  kGravity     520 px/s^2

  hangTime   = 2 * 210 / 520 = 0.808 s
  jumpDist   = 95 * 0.808     = 76.7 px = 4.80 tiles
  jumpHeight = 210^2 / (2*520)= 42.4 px = 2.65 tiles

Level rules
  Mandatory pits without Bolt Step: <= 3 tiles  (floor(4.80)-1)
  Coin platforms: height <= 2 tiles above stance
  One optional wider pit may require Bolt Step; a platform route keeps the exit clearable
  Enemy spacing: stand 2+ tiles away so 32 px spear reach hits before body overlap

Combat reach (src/Combat.cpp)
  Gale hurtbox stays 10x16
  Tempest Strike hitbox: from AABB edge, 32 px forward (40 with Wider Strike), body height
  Active during thrust (anim in [0.10, 0.36) of a 0.44 s attack), re-tested each tick
  Cyclone: ring in front and around; still no body-overlap required
  Enemy contact damage: THEIR body vs Gale body only
