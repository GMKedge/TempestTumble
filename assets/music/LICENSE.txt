gm.sf2 — Tempest GM wavetable bank
==================================

This General MIDI SoundFont was generated by tools/make_gm_sf2.py for
Tempest Tumble (and the same teachable MIDI stack intended for Sprout and
Steel). Every sample is a synthesized wavetable or decaying burst made
for this file. Nothing was copied from commercial or third-party banks.
