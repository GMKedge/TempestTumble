gm.sf2
======
TimGM6mb SoundFont by Tim Brechbill (2004), later shipped with MuseScore 1.3.
Vendored from the same bank used in Sprout and Steel.
License: GNU General Public License, version 2 (GPL-2.0-only)
Size ~5.7 MiB. Complete GM (128 programs + drums).

You may replace gm.sf2 with any other GM bank (FluidR3 MIT is ~140 MiB).
Keep this file in sync with the bank you ship.
